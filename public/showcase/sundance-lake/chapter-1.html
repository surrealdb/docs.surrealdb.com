How to read a conversation. Each message is what a person said or wrote. In email and chat, their assistant reviews a draft before it is sent. In a call or a meeting, nobody is interrupted: the assistant reviews what its owner said and leaves a note, which they may notice a minute later.

Project records
PO 4500087312 to Muskeg Mainline Constructors 3 July 2025, 10:00

PO 4500087312 issued to Muskeg Mainline Constructors Ltd. (vendor 201174) for Sundance Lake Loop mainline construction, lump sum $88,200,000 plus unit rates. Rate sheet: crew spread standby $38,400 per day. Weather standby is payable only after 48 consecutive hours of stoppage directed by Chinook; the first 48 hours are at the contractor's risk. Cold-weather clause: work stops when the temperature is below -40 °C, and that stoppage counts as weather standby. Extra work only on a signed change order, except field work the Chinook PM approves within his limit and confirms in writing within 5 working days. Invoices monthly against progress, 10% holdback released 60 days after in-service.
