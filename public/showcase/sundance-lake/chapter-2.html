Field report: hydrotest water source 28 August 2025, 14:00

Field report, 28 August. The planned hydrostatic test water source at Gully Creek pond is too shallow after a dry summer: about 40% of the required volume. The nearest alternative with enough water is Sundance Lake, 6 km north of the right-of-way. There is no road access to its south shore in summer; a winter road can reach it after freeze-up. The certificate requires an aquatic survey of any new test water source, filed 60 days before construction.
