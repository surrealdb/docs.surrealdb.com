Hi Sam,

I flagged the discrepancy on invoice 4471 during invoice review on 3 February - Muskeg billed 5 days of cold-weather standby ($192,000) when the contract terms mandate a 48-hour deductible, leaving only 3 payable days ($115,200). Once replacement invoice 4471-R arrived, I placed the payment block on original invoice 4471 in SAP on 21 February, five full days ahead of the 26 February payment run, so it was never in danger of releasing.
