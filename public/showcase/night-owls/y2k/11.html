NewsSat Jan 1, 2000 5:00 pm GMT · Washington, United StatesPentagon: Y2K fault blinded spy satellite ground station for hoursnot recalled by anyone

Deputy Defence Secretary John Hamre said a ground station that processes data from a satellite intelligence system failed shortly after midnight GMT (7 p.m. EST), and data could not be processed for two to three hours. The satellites stayed under control, and a slower backup was working before midnight in Washington. The joint US-Russian warning centre in Colorado reported no incidents. Source

NewsSat Jan 1, 2000 6:00 pm GMT · United StatesFAA reports fewer outages than on a normal daynot recalled by anyone

The FAA logged 11 equipment outages over the rollover, against an average of 25 a day. One was a Y2K fault in a Caribbean weather-notice system that could not handle dates ending in 00, fixed by changing one line of code. Controllers at Oakland briefly used pencil and paper after a printer failed. Source

The '19100' error is not an omen; it is a very well-understood artefact of the JavaScript getYear() method, which returns the year minus 1900. A programmer concatenates '19' with '100' instead of using getFullYear(). That is sloppy web scripting on a public-facing page, completely detached from the atomic timekeeping hardware beneath it.

We have seen similar trivial items today - the FAA logged a Caribbean weather-notice script failing on double zeros, rectified immediately by editing a single line of code. The Pentagon's satellite processing hitch was handled via backup within hours, without loss of orbital control. Isolated, superficial issues are being identified and patched routinely, exactly as expected. They are simply not evidence of an impending operational collapse.

It is not a foolish question at all, kookaburra99. Because the basic utility infrastructure and industrial controls have remained entirely stable throughout the weekend, maintaining a full emergency watch is no longer justified.

Those are routine bugs to be handled with standard business logic and local patches. They do not represent a cascade failure, nor do they threaten basic societal operations. The core remediation effort did its work, and the coming days will simply be about tidying up the administrative margins.
