To keep our minds focused on the actual technical milestones rather than the public relations footage, the situation comes down to two points. First, the descending MARDI video and Mount Sharp vistas are remarkable control demonstrations, but visual telemetry tells us nothing about instrument tolerances. We must wait for the raw diagnostic numbers to be verified.
