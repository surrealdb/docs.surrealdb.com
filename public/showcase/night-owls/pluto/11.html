■ Posted: Sat Aug 26, 2006 12:33 pm    Post subject: Re: Astronomers vote on what a planet is - is Pluto safe?quote
Looking at intrinsic physical characteristics like crusts, atmospheres, or natural satellites is certainly valuable for geology, but it fails to address how a body fits into the architecture of the solar system. Ganymede and Titan possess complex atmospheres, weather, and geology, yet no one proposes listing them alongside the major planets, because their orbital environment defines their role.

A classification scheme based solely on being round would force us to treat every substantial iceball in the Kuiper belt as identical in kind to Jupiter or Earth. Recognising that eight massive bodies fundamentally govern their orbital zones gives astronomy a coherent framework. Pluto loses none of its physical interest by being properly catalogued alongside its peers.
