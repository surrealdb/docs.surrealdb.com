■ Posted: Mon Dec 31, 2001 7:04 am    Post subject: Re: Euro notes and coins arrive on 1 January - who's ready?quote
kookaburra99 wrote:
Shops will just hand back old francs if they run out of euros, nobody gets turned away over change.
Giving change in the legacy currency is precisely what the dual-circulation guidelines permit when euro coin reserves run low. Retailers are asked to provide change in euros wherever feasible to speed the transition, but no shopkeeper is required to abandon a sale merely because their euro drawer is temporarily depleted. In France, the franc remains legal tender until 17 February, which gives merchants ample legal leeway to handle ordinary transactions during any temporary delivery bottleneck.

Furthermore, the industrial dispute in France concerns branch counters, whereas automated teller networks operate independently of bank teller hours. The cash machines have already been preloaded, and automated replenishment does not depend on counter clerks being at their desks on Wednesday morning. Here in Rotterdam, the banks have taken sensible precautions with their equipment, and businesses have had ample advance delivery of coin rolls to manage their floats. A union dispute across the border will not arrest the physical machinery of retail commerce.
