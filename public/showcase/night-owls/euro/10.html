Actually, the precise figure provided by the European Central Bank is approximately 197,400 terminals, so 'nearly two hundred thousand' is an acceptable approximation, though one should not round prematurely.

Wim Duisenberg noted today that the changeover is proceeding even beyond official expectations, which is supported by the rapid uptake across retail networks. While commentators in the United Kingdom like to debate political referendums, the administrative reality is that cross-border cash settlements are occurring without systemic clearance friction.

The security features on the genuine notes are functioning as intended. The mechanical infrastructure has proven itself sound, and routine daily commerce is absorbing the new denomination perfectly well.
