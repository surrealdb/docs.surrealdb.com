Punchcard's comparison to a mainframe parallel run captures the situation neatly. The European Central Bank confirmed today that over 80% of cash machines across the euro area dispensed euros on the very first day, reaching 100% here in the Netherlands, as well as in Germany, Austria, and Luxembourg.

When consumers present large banknotes at a toll booth or counter, local coin reserves naturally deplete rapidly. This is why board member Domingo Solans specifically requested that the public tender exact change where feasible. Until till floats accumulate sufficient small metal denominations, returning previous national currency fulfills the transaction cleanly. The dual circulation architecture was designed for this exact purpose.

Commerce adapts at its own sensible pace.
