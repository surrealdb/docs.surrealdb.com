Shared and private memory in one Context

The news and the forum are shared scopes that every member reads. Each member also has a private scope for notes to self: predictions, bets, and opinions of the others. The private notes sit in the same Context as everything else, yet each one came back only to the member who wrote it.

Across all five threads, private notes were returned 7044 times, every time to their owner, and 0 times to anyone else. Separate per-user stores would each need their own copy of the forum; one shared index could not keep the notes private.

How the board grew closer

How warmly each member felt about each other member, read from the feelings recorded before every post. In the first three threads TheyKnow, the conspiracy theorist, is at odds with everyone and they are at odds with him. From 2012 he warms to almost everyone, while the others come round more slowly. Nothing in the setup asked for this: it comes from what the members remembered about each other from one thread to the next.
