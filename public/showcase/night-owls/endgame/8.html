Re: Avengers: Endgame - NO SPOILERS please

by OffByOne » Sat Apr 27, 2019 6:47 am

PintOfMild wrote:All those 24-hour showings are doing the heavy lifting right now, cramming a month of cinema visits into four days.
The arithmetic of cinema programming makes this inevitable. An auditorium running a three-hour title can manage at most four screenings per screen each day, whereas shorter releases easily achieve five or six. What we are seeing now in the reports from North America and China - round-the-clock screenings at three and six in the morning - is an emergency measure by theatre managers to capture the initial surge. Commercial exhibitors cannot maintain twenty-four-hour operating rosters indefinitely without incurring substantial staffing overheads, nor will ordinary audiences tolerate early morning slots once work resumes on Monday.

When those artificial time-slots disappear and the pre-booked enthusiasts return home, daily capacity will decline sharply. A film requires broad, sustained general interest across months to approach historical totals, not merely a concentrated burst of loyalty. That fundamental friction in the mechanics of exhibition guarantees the post-weekend returns will look ordinary. My own visit to the cinema here in Rotterdam will certainly wait until those evening auditoriums return to sensible capacity levels next week.
