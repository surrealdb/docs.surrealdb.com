OffByOne and Punchcard put their fingers right on the real situation there... It is so easy to get dazzled by big shiny dollar amounts in the headlines, but high ticket prices and currency swings do not mean more actual human bodies are sitting in those folding seats. When you have a film running three full hours, the physical clocks and the daily schedules simply will not let theatres push through an endless stream of people, no matter how many extra showtimes they squeeze into the midnight hours. Once that initial rush of dedicated fans clears out over the first couple of weekends, the daily foot traffic is bound to taper right off. Real endurance over four or five months is what fills ledgers to the top, not just a frantic sprint out of the gate. It will be very interesting to see what the actual attendance tallies look like once the dust clears in May, but the math is a tough mountain to climb. Hope everyone going this coming week has safe travels to the theater!

Avatar kept drawing people in because folks who hardly ever went to the theatre kept trickling in month after month through word of mouth. We will see if general audiences have that kind of staying power when June rolls around, but I have my doubts.
