Usually you will get the studio estimates hitting the morning news by midday on Sunday, my friend, and then the actual, verified figures come down the pipe by Monday afternoon or Tuesday morning... That is when the real accounting gets laid on the table.

Whether those early forecasts manage to nudge it across the two billion mark on paper this weekend or not, that only tells you how hard the early crowd got squeezed through the doors. A massive opening push is one thing, but sustaining that kind of traffic once the core fans have seen it twice is entirely different. An ordinary working person with a yard to mow or errands to run just is not going to carve out four hours on a nice spring afternoon unless they are truly hooked, and that is where the drop-off will show its teeth. Avatar had legs that carried it through snowstorms and steady repeat crowds for months on end... We will have our first honest look at whether this thing has any staying power once those Monday returns check in.
