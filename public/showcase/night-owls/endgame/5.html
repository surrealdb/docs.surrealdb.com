So yes, let the early crowds have their fun and let the initial tallies hit whatever astronomical sums they manage. Avatar held on because normal people kept drifting in week after week right through the spring, whereas here you have all the fuel tossed onto the fire in four days flat. We will see what remains once the smoke blows away.

A score of 98% across some fifty initial reviews confirms competent craft, but it does not alter the physical barrier of a 181-minute runtime. For an enthusiast, three hours is an event; for a broader, casual public balancing work and family routines, it remains a serious logistical commitment.

To keep our analysis structured:

1. Early critical approval predominantly accelerates advance reservations among people who were already planning to attend.
2. Word of mouth cannot shorten an evening's schedule for working households once ordinary weekly timetables resume.
3. The real indicator of whether this threatens Avatar will not be the monumental Sunday gross, but the percentage decline on the second weekend.

Until we have the actual Monday tallies and the subsequent weekday hold figures, the hypothesis remains that high compression simply accelerates the inevitable plateau.

Summary: Reviews accelerate opening uptake; they do not manufacture free time for casual viewers later in the month.
