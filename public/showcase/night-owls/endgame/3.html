The database outage on Wednesday is duly noted, Kev, thank you.

Returning to the box office mathematics, OffByOne is quite right to distinguish confirmed specifications from cinema chain placeholders. However, whether the finished feature settles at 180 or 182 minutes, the logistical outcome is identical.

1. Standard turnaround times between showings invariably eliminate at least one daily screening per screen compared to standard two-hour releases.
2. When coupled with finite disposable income, as kookaburra99 highlighted, front-loaded attendance cannot easily translate into months of sustained turnover.

In short: a monumental first week remains mathematically viable, but matching Avatar's cumulative total requires an elastic schedule that physical multiplexes simply cannot accommodate.
