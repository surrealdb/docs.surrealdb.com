Re: Avengers: Endgame - NO SPOILERS please

by quasistatic » Tue Apr 30, 2019 7:00 pm

The reported figures for Monday have now begun to filter through, and they warrant careful examination. According to trade reporting this afternoon, the domestic take reached $36.7 million on Monday. That places the global cumulative total at roughly $1.34 billion across its first six days.

While that is undeniably a formidable sum for a non-holiday weekday, we ought to maintain our focus on the fundamental arithmetic rather than immediate hyperbole. Two observations are worth keeping in mind:

1. A portion of that Monday gross inevitably represents deferred spillover from patrons who found weekend sessions entirely sold out, rather than newly converted casual viewers.

2. The structural barrier discussed above remains fully intact. A three-hour running time - expanding to nearly four hours once adverts, previews, and seating are accounted for - remains an enormous hurdle for ordinary working households on a Tuesday or Wednesday evening.

Avatar achieved its unprecedented endurance because uncommitted patrons trickled in steadily for months on end. Clearing early demand at extraordinary speed is quite distinct from sustaining broad public appeal into early summer. Let us see how the numbers settle once the initial rush is truly exhausted.

The actual settlement of thirty-six million for Monday is certainly a healthy return, but quasistatic puts his finger right on the crux of the matter.

When a release sells out an entire weekend, a great many eager patrons simply take the next available chair on Monday evening. That is deferred business, not necessarily fresh footfall from the wider public.

A working week is five days long, not one. We will get a much clearer picture of whether ordinary folks are genuinely turning out once Tuesday and Wednesday pass through the books. There is no need to jump to conclusions until the dust settles.
