Re: Avengers: Endgame - NO SPOILERS please

by quasistatic » Thu May 2, 2019 10:33 pm

The social media metrics reported today certainly illustrate that climate. Twitter noted over fifty million tweets regarding the film since January, with almost half generated during this past week alone. That level of intense online noise creates an obvious urgency among regular viewers to protect themselves from plot revelations before the working week concludes.

However, urgency should not be mistaken for broader demographic expansion. What we are witnessing is simply a very wide, very anxious core audience distributing itself across five or six days rather than three. That process naturally props up mid-week box office tallies, but it remains finite by its very nature. Once that protective barrier of early viewings is established, the film must rely on ordinary word of mouth and repeat attendances from people who do not mind giving up an entire afternoon or evening.

Surpassing a worldwide total of over two and a half billion dollars requires casual cinemagoers to turn out steadily well into June and July. We should have the first reliable sense of whether that footfall actually exists when the second weekend returns are tallied.
