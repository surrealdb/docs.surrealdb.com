VentStream is an open-core CDC engine that keeps SurrealDB continuously in
sync with an existing operational database — PostgreSQL, MySQL, MongoDB, or
Neo4j. Declare joins once and normalized rows arrive as composed SurrealDB
documents: an order with its customer embedded and its items collected,
kept honest through updates, deletes, key changes, and truncates. Declared
HNSW vector indexes are ensured at startup, so synced embedding fields are
KNN-searchable the moment they land, and every write is an ordinary commit —
`LIVE SELECT` subscribers get real-time push for free.

There is a live demo at
[surreal-demo.ventstream.dev](https://surreal-demo.ventstream.dev): every
button writes plain SQL to Postgres, and the browser watches composed
documents update over a WebSocket `LIVE SELECT`, typically in under half a
second — including join fan-out, where boosting one customer row re-composes
every order document embedding it.
