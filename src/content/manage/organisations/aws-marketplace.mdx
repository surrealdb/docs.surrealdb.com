# AWS Marketplace

AWS Marketplace is an alternative route to paying for managed instances: you subscribe through the Marketplace listing, and charges appear on your existing AWS bill rather than on a card held by SurrealDB.

This matters where procurement already runs through AWS — consolidated invoicing across vendors, spend drawn down against an existing AWS commitment, or a purchasing process that only approves Marketplace listings. Everything else about the instances is identical.

## Subscribing

1. Open the **SurrealDB Cloud** listing in [AWS Marketplace](https://aws.amazon.com/marketplace) and select **Subscribe**, then confirm the contract and pricing terms.
2. Complete the AWS account and payment linkage as prompted. The subscription is billed through AWS.
3. Follow the set-up steps shown after subscribing to connect the purchase to your organisation. This involves signing in to your SurrealDB account and authorising the link.

Once the link is in place, [deploy instances](/docs/manage/instances/create) exactly as you would otherwise. Plans, instance types, regions, and capabilities are the same; only the invoicing route differs.

## Where your invoices are

Invoices and payment information for a Marketplace subscription live in the **AWS Billing and Cost Management** console. They do not appear in SurrealDB Studio, so the [Billing](/docs/manage/organisations/billing) page shows no invoices for a Marketplace-billed organisation.

Usage figures are still visible in Studio under **Usage** — see [Usage and spend](/docs/manage/organisations/billing#usage-and-spend) — which is how you reconcile what the AWS charge covers.

## Changing or cancelling

Changes and cancellations go through AWS, following AWS's own guidance for Marketplace subscriptions.

> [!WARNING]
> Cancelling the Marketplace subscription removes the billing route for the organisation. Make sure the organisation is on a valid plan, with its own payment method if needed, before cancelling — otherwise running instances are left without one.
