# Enterprise members

The **Members** page lists everyone who belongs to the enterprise, along with the roles each of them holds.

Two tabs divide the list. **Members** holds the people who have joined. **Pending** holds the invitations you have sent that nobody has accepted yet.

Where a member came from is shown beside their name. A member who signed in through one of your own [single sign-on](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/single-sign-on) connections is marked as such, which separates them from a member holding an ordinary SurrealDB account.

> [!NOTE]
> Once single sign-on is configured, your identity provider decides who may authenticate. This page grants and revokes access inside SurrealDB, and it does not change your directory. Remove someone from your identity provider to stop them signing in at all.

## Invite one member

1. Open **Members** in your enterprise.
2. Select **Invite member**.
3. Enter the email address of the person you invite.
4. Choose the [roles](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/roles) they hold.
5. Send the invitation.

The invitation appears under **Pending** until it is accepted. Select **Revoke invitation** to withdraw one you no longer want.

Invite the address of a named person rather than a shared mailbox. A shared mailbox hides which person acted, and you cannot withdraw it from one reader who leaves.

## Import a member list

Use an import when you have many people to invite at once.

1. Open **Members**, then start an import.
2. Choose the file that holds the addresses.
3. Choose the **Default roles**, which apply to any row that names no roles of its own.
4. Check the rows Studio has read.
5. Send the invitations.

Studio reads the whole file and shows you every row before anything is sent, with the roles it resolved for each one. A row it cannot invite is listed with the reason, so you can correct the file and import it again.

### The file format

The file holds one member per line, as an email address followed by the roles that member holds.

```text title="members.csv"
email,roles
alex@example.com,Engineering
sam@example.com,Engineering;Billing
jo@example.com,
```

Three rules govern how the file is read:

- A **header row** is optional. With one, the columns are named. Without one, each line is read as the address first and the roles second.
- **Roles in one cell are separated by semicolons**, because the comma already separates the columns.
- A **row that names no roles** takes the default roles you chose.

A row is reported rather than sent when the address is not valid, when it names a role the enterprise does not hold, or when that person is already a member or already invited.

> [!WARNING]
> Commas separate columns, and the file is read without quoted fields. An address or a role name containing a comma is read as two columns and the row fails.

## Change what a member holds

1. Find the member in the list.
2. Open the member actions.
3. Select the roles they hold, then save.

Selecting roles replaces the whole set that member held, rather than adding to it. Include every role you want them to keep.

## Remove a member

Open the member actions to remove the membership. The person keeps their account and their access to anything outside this enterprise.

Remove access when a person leaves the project, and review the member list at regular intervals. A member who has left keeps every role you granted them until you remove the membership.
