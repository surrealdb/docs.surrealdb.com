# Enterprise organisations

An enterprise adds single sign-on, custom roles, and central member management to an organisation, so everyone at your company signs in with your own credentials and holds the access you grant them.

A standard [organisation](/docs/manage/organisations) groups instances, members, usage, and billing, and gives each member one of three fixed roles. An enterprise keeps all of that and adds a second layer of administration above it, with its own members, its own roles, and a connection to your identity provider.

> [!NOTE]
> An enterprise organisation is a different product from [Enterprise Edition](/docs/manage/enterprise). Enterprise Edition is the SurrealDB server sold with a licence key, which adds capabilities such as audit logging and FIPS mode to the database itself. An enterprise organisation changes how people sign in to SurrealDB Studio and what they may administer there.

## What an enterprise adds

| Capability | What it gives you |
| --- | --- |
| **[Single sign-on](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/single-sign-on)** | People sign in through your own identity provider instead of creating a SurrealDB account. |
| **[Custom roles](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/roles)** | Roles you compose yourself, rather than the three fixed organisation roles. |
| **[Central member management](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/members)** | One member list for the whole company, with invitations sent one at a time or from a file. |
| **Sign-in branding** | Your logo and colours on the page where people sign in. |

## Get an enterprise

You cannot create an enterprise yourself. SurrealDB arranges one with you, because single sign-on and the plan that carries it are set up on our side.

1. Open [SurrealDB Studio](https://studio.surrealdb.com).
2. Open the account menu, then select **Enterprises**.
3. Select **Request an enterprise**.
4. Describe what you need, then send the message.

The SurrealDB team will then reply to arrange the plan.

## Where each part is administered

An enterprise is administered in a section of its own, separate from the organisation view, at `/enterprises` in Studio. Select **Return to Studio** to go back to your organisations and instances.

| Page | What you do there |
| --- | --- |
| **[Members](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/members)** | See who belongs to the enterprise, invite people, and assign roles. |
| **[Roles](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/roles)** | Compose roles out of the permissions an enterprise can grant. |
| **[Single sign-on](/docs/manage/organisations/enterprise/single-sign-on)** | Verify your email domains and connect your identity provider. |
| **Settings** | The display name, the identifiers, and the sign-in branding. |

Every page stays listed whatever your access. A page you cannot read names the permission it needs, so you know what to ask an owner for.

## Settings and branding

**Settings** holds the identity of the enterprise, and the values other systems refer to it by.

| Field | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| **Display name** | The name the enterprise is shown under, across SurrealDB products and on the sign-in page. |
| **Identifier** | The short name used to refer to the enterprise in configuration. |
| **Enterprise ID** | The generated id. It is read-only, and you copy it rather than retype it. |
| **Logo URL** | A publicly reachable image, shown on sign-in and in Studio. |
| **Primary colour** | The colour of the buttons on the sign-in page. |
| **Page background** | The background colour of the sign-in page. |

Be sure to quote the enterprise id when you raise a [support ticket](/docs/manage/organisations/support). The id names one enterprise exactly, where a display name might not.
