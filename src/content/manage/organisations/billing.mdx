# Billing

Billing happens at the organisation level, not per instance. Compute, storage, and network use across every instance in the organisation roll up into one monthly invoice, and charges are based on what is provisioned and used rather than a flat licence fee.

You are invoiced monthly for the previous month's usage. The cycle runs from the first day of the month to the last, and the invoice arrives by email. Prices on the website exclude VAT and sales tax; applicable taxes appear on the invoice. Current rates are on the [pricing page](https://surrealdb.com/pricing).

> [!IMPORTANT] Only organisation **Owners** can change billing and payment details. Owners and **Admins** can view invoices. See [Members and roles](/docs/manage/organisations/members-and-roles).

## Billing and payment details

Open the organisation in [SurrealDB Studio](https://app.surrealdb.com) and go to **Billing**.

![The Billing page for the Acme Corp organisation in SurrealDB Studio, showing a Billing details card with the name Alex Doe and email alex@example.com, a Payment details card listing a Mastercard ending in 4242, each with an Edit button, and a Spectron plan section below with options to upgrade, cancel, or view pricing.](~/assets/img/surrealdb/manage/organisation-billing.webp)

**Billing details** hold the name and email address that appear on invoices. **Payment details** hold the payment method. Select **Edit** on either to change it.

Use a billing address that finance will recognise, and an address that reaches more than one person — an invoice sent to someone who has left the company goes unnoticed until an instance is suspended.

If your organisation has a VAT or tax identification number, enter it in the billing details so it appears on every invoice.

## Discount codes

Discount codes are applied in the **Discount codes** section of the same page. A code applies to subsequent invoices for the organisation, not retrospectively.

## Other subscriptions

The Billing page also holds subscriptions for other SurrealDB products in the organisation, such as [Spectron](/docs/spectron) context packages. Each has its own plan card, with actions to change or cancel it. They share the organisation's payment method and appear on the same invoice.

## Invoices

Invoices are listed under **Billing**, each showing its date, status, and amount, with a link to view or download the document itself.

Invoices are retained for the organisation even after the instances that generated them are deleted, so an audit or an expense query can still be answered later.

## Usage and spend

**Usage** breaks down what produced the charges before the invoice arrives, for the current or the previous month.

![The Usage page for the Acme Corp organisation in SurrealDB Studio, with a Previous month and Current month toggle, cards showing total spend, total compute hours, total storage, and instance count for the selected period, and a Usage breakdown section listing individual ledger entries.](~/assets/img/surrealdb/manage/organisation-usage.webp)

The four cards give the totals — spend, compute hours, storage, and instance count. **Usage breakdown** lists the individual ledger entries behind them, which is where you look when a total is higher than expected: it attributes the charge to a period and a resource.

Check it after resizing an instance. A larger instance type is billed on what is provisioned, so the effect on spend appears immediately rather than at the end of the month. See [Scaling](/docs/manage/instances/scaling).

`surrealctl org spend` and `surrealctl org usage` print the same figures for scripting or for feeding an internal cost report. See [surrealctl organisations](/docs/manage/surrealctl/organisations).

## Controlling what you spend

- **[Pause](/docs/manage/instances/configure#pause-an-instance)** non-production instances when they are idle. A paused instance keeps its data and stops accruing usage charges.
- **Right-size rather than over-provision.** Storage can only ever be increased, so a generous initial allocation is a permanent cost.
- **Delete instances you have finished with**, after taking an [export](/docs/manage/instances/import-and-export) if the data still matters.

## AWS Marketplace subscriptions

If you subscribed through AWS Marketplace, billing runs entirely through your AWS account. Invoices and payment details are in the **AWS Billing and Cost Management** console and do not appear in Studio. See [AWS Marketplace](/docs/manage/organisations/aws-marketplace).

## Billing questions

Email [support@surrealdb.com](mailto:support@surrealdb.com) with the details of your query. Write from the address associated with your account — you can confirm which that is from the account menu in the top right of SurrealDB Studio — so the request can be matched to the organisation.

For technical rather than billing questions, see [Support](/docs/manage/organisations/support).
