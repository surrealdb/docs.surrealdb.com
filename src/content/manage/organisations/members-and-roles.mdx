# Members and roles

Membership is managed from the **Team** section of the organisation in [SurrealDB Studio](https://app.surrealdb.com). Each member holds exactly one role, and that role decides what they can see and change across every instance in the organisation.

![The Team page for the Acme Corp organisation in SurrealDB Studio, showing Members and Pending tabs with one pending invitation, a member search box, an Invite member button, and a single row for Alex Doe at alex@example.com labelled OWNER and YOU.](~/assets/img/surrealdb/manage/organisation-members.webp)

The **Members** tab lists people who have accepted; **Pending** lists invitations that have been sent but not yet accepted.

## Inviting members

1. Open **Team** and select **Invite member**.
2. Enter the person's email address and choose the role they should hold.
3. Send the invitation. It appears under **Pending** until they accept.

The invitee creates an account, if they do not already have one, and joins the organisation on the role you chose. See [Accounts and sign-in](/docs/manage/organisations/sign-in).

Invite named individual addresses rather than shared mailboxes, particularly for Admin and Owner. A shared mailbox makes an action untraceable to a person and cannot be revoked when one of its readers leaves.

`surrealctl invite` sends invitations and `surrealctl team list` reports current membership, which is what you want when reviewing access across several organisations. See [surrealctl organisations](/docs/manage/surrealctl/organisations).

## What each role can do

The creator of an organisation is its **Owner**. Owners can invite members as **Admin** or **Member**.

| Action | Owner | Admin | Member |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| View instances and team | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| Deploy, pause, and delete instances | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change instance configuration and [capabilities](/docs/manage/instances/configure#capabilities) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Invite and remove members | Yes | Yes | Own membership only |
| Change another member's role | Yes | No | No |
| View [invoices](/docs/manage/organisations/billing) | Yes | Yes | No |
| Change billing and payment details | Yes | No | No |

In short: an Admin runs the organisation's infrastructure but does not control its money or its access list; a Member is read-only apart from being able to leave.

> [!NOTE] Roles are fixed sets today. Custom roles and per-user granular permissions are planned.

## Working with least privilege

Grant the lowest role that still lets someone do their job, and revisit the list rather than treating it as set once.

Three actions deserve particular attention, because each can cause loss or exposure that is hard to reverse:

- **Deleting an instance** destroys its data — see [Configure an instance](/docs/manage/instances/configure#delete-an-instance).
- **Changing [network access](/docs/manage/instances/network-access)** decides which external destinations a query may reach.
- **Restoring a [backup](/docs/manage/instances/backups)** creates a new instance holding a copy of production data.

All three are available to every Admin and Owner. If you need people who can operate instances without holding those permissions, the boundary to use is a separate organisation, not a lower role.

Remove access when someone leaves the project. A departed member with an Admin role remains an Admin until removed.
