# Referrals

The referral programme rewards you for introducing new users. Each account gets a personal referral link, and rewards range from account credit to items from the SurrealDB store.

## How it works

1. Copy your personal referral link from the [referrals page](https://app.surrealdb.com/referrals).
2. Share it with whoever you are recommending SurrealDB to.
3. When they sign up through the link, the reward is credited to your account.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/referrals.png" alt="The referrals page in SurrealDB Studio, showing the personal referral link to share and the rewards earned from sign-ups so far." />

The same page tracks sign-ups attributed to your link and the rewards they have earned. Credits apply to your organisation's future invoices — see [Billing](/docs/manage/organisations/billing).
