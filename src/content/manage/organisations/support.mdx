# Support plans and raising a ticket

SurrealDB offers three paid support plans: Standard, Business Critical and Premium. Each commits to published initial response targets, from 8 business hours for a Sev (Severity) 1 on Standard to 30 minutes on Premium. A plan applies to the whole organisation and is added to an annual contract.

Community help is free and open to everyone. A support plan is what lets you raise a technical ticket. Account and billing tickets are open to every organisation, with or without a plan.

## Support plans

Each plan sets the coverage hours, the number of named contacts, and the channels your organisation can reach support through.

| | Community (free) | Standard | Business Critical | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- | --- |
| **For** | Anyone building on SurrealDB | Development teams | Production applications | Mission-critical applications |
| **Coverage** | Best effort, no commitment | 09:00 to 17:00, business days | 24x7 | 24x7 |
| **[Response-time targets](#initial-response-targets)** | None | Yes | Yes | Yes |
| **Named contacts** | Anyone on your team | 5 | 10 | 20 |
| **Channels** | Discord | Tickets via the SurrealDB Studio support portal | Tickets via the SurrealDB Studio support portal | Tickets via the SurrealDB Studio support portal, plus a dedicated Slack or Teams channel |
| **Technical account manager** | No | No | No | Yes |

Plans are priced as a percentage of your annual contract and can be added to an organisation at any time. To add one, [enquire about paid support](https://surrealdb.com/contact) or speak to your account manager.

With a plan in place, **Support** in the organisation sidebar shows your plan and every ticket for the organisation. Without one, it shows **View plans**, which opens the [pricing page](https://surrealdb.com/pricing).

## Severity levels

You choose the severity when you raise a ticket. Choose it against business impact rather than against how urgent the work feels.

| Severity | Definition |
| --- | --- |
| Sev 1 | Production is down or unusable, or production data is at risk. No workaround. |
| Sev 2 | Production is running in reduced capacity. No workaround, or the workaround is not sustainable. |
| Sev 3 | A fault with a workaround available, or a non-production environment is affected. |
| Sev 4 | A question, a documentation issue, or a feature request. No operational impact. |

## Initial response targets

| Severity | Standard | Business Critical | Premium |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| Sev 1 | 8 business hours | 2 hours | 30 minutes |
| Sev 2 | 12 business hours | 4 hours | 1 hour |
| Sev 3 | 24 business hours | 8 business hours | 4 business hours |
| Sev 4 | 24 business hours | 24 business hours | 24 business hours |

An initial response target is the time to the first substantive response from a SurrealDB support engineer. Targets are not guarantees, and they are not times to resolution.

Business hours are 09:00 to 17:00 on business days in your organisation's assigned support region, which is set in your contract.

## What a support plan covers

A support plan covers every SurrealDB product on your contract, including SurrealDB, SurrealDB Cloud and Agent Memory, whether you run them in the cloud or self-hosted. In scope:

- Faults and unexpected behaviour in the product
- Configuration, deployment, upgrade and migration questions
- Performance triage, including query and index behaviour
- Availability, backup and recovery for Cloud instances

Forward-deployed engineers are available to work alongside your team on deeper pieces of work, scoped separately from your support plan. Speak to your account manager.

Support covers the software SurrealDB provides. Infrastructure and third-party services from other suppliers sit outside it.

## Raise a ticket

Tickets are raised from SurrealDB Studio. A paid support plan is required to raise a technical ticket. Account and billing tickets are open to every organisation.

1. Open **Support** in the organisation sidebar.
2. Select **New ticket**.
3. Enter a **Subject** that names the problem.
4. Describe the problem under **What is your reason for contacting us?**
5. Choose the **Organisation** the ticket belongs to.
6. Choose the **Severity level**.
7. Select **Submit**.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/create-support-ticket-form.png" alt="The Create new ticket dialog in SurrealDB Studio, with a note that replies are sent to the ticket and to your email address, a Subject field reading I have an issue with my functions, a reason field describing an unresponsive instance, an Organisation selector, a Severity level selector set to partial loss of functionality affecting some users or features, a note that attachments are included by replying to the ticket, and a Submit button." />

In the description, state what you expected, what happened, and when the problem started. Include the [instance id](/docs/manage/instances/configure#the-instance-id) and the [organisation id](/docs/manage/organisations#organisation-settings), which you can copy from Studio. Both save a round trip.

For a Sev 1, also include the time the incident began, the error returned, and whether the instance is reachable. Response targets run from the moment the ticket is submitted.

Studio opens the ticket after you submit it. Replies appear in the ticket and also go to your email address. To attach a log, a query, or a screenshot, add it to a reply.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/create-support-ticket-page.png" alt="A submitted support ticket in SurrealDB Studio, showing the description and severity level on the left, a panel on the right with the state Submitted, the type Standard and the time of the last update, an updates timeline recording the state change, a reply box with an attachment button, and an All tickets button at the top right." />

**All tickets** at the top right of the **Support** section lists every open and closed ticket for the organisation.

## Escalation and incidents

To escalate, raise a new ticket at the severity the problem now warrants. Alerting is triggered by new tickets, so a reply on an existing ticket raises no alert. Where the new ticket covers the same problem, say so in the description and the two can be merged once the issue is resolved.

For platform-wide incidents, check the [status page](https://status.surrealdb.com) before raising a ticket.

## Account and billing questions

Every organisation can raise an account or billing ticket in SurrealDB Studio, with or without a paid support plan. Open **Support** in the organisation sidebar and select **New ticket**.

You can also write to [support@surrealdb.com](mailto:support@surrealdb.com) from the address associated with your account. See [Billing](/docs/manage/organisations/billing).

## Community support

The [SurrealDB Discord](https://discord.gg/surrealdb) answers most questions. `#surrealdb-cloud` covers managed instances, and `#help` or `#general` cover everything else. SurrealDB Ambassadors and the SurrealDB team read the server and escalate the issues that need it.

Use the community for questions that carry no confidential detail. Raise a ticket for anything that involves your data, your credentials, or your billing. Community help carries no response-time commitment.

## Report a security issue

Send a security report to [security@surrealdb.com](mailto:security@surrealdb.com) rather than to Discord or a ticket. For a vulnerability in SurrealDB itself, submit a report through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/surrealdb/surrealdb/security/advisories) rather than a public issue. The disclosure policy is in the [FAQs](/docs/manage/organisations/faqs#how-do-i-report-a-security-issue).
