# Support

Support comes in three forms: the community, an AI assistant trained on public material, and paid support plans with response-time commitments. The first two are free and available to everyone; the third is added to an organisation.

## Community support

The [SurrealDB Discord](https://discord.gg/surrealdb) is where most questions get answered. `#surrealdb-cloud` covers managed instances, and `#help` or `#general` cover everything else. The engineering team reads the server and escalates issues that need it.

Use the community for questions with no confidential detail. Anything involving your data, your credentials, or your billing belongs in a ticket instead.

## Surreal Sidekick

Surreal Sidekick is an AI assistant in SurrealDB Studio. It answers questions about SurrealDB, converts queries into SurrealQL, and points at the relevant documentation. It is trained on public material only — the [documentation](/docs), [SurrealDB University](/learn), and the [SurrealDB Book](/learn/book) — so it knows the product but nothing about your instances.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/sidekick.png" alt="The Surreal Sidekick panel in SurrealDB Studio, showing a conversational prompt for asking questions about SurrealDB and SurrealQL." />

Open it from the sidebar and ask.

> [!WARNING]
> Like any language model, Sidekick can be confidently wrong. Verify anything consequential against the documentation, and do not submit sensitive data to it.

## Paid support plans

A support plan is what lets you raise a ticket, and it applies to the whole organisation.

Open **Support** in the organisation sidebar. Without a plan you see **View plans**, which leads to the [pricing page](/pricing); adding a plan involves contacting the support team. With a plan, the same section shows the plan and any open tickets.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/add-support-plan.png" alt="The Support section of an organisation in SurrealDB Studio, showing the option to view available support plans before one has been added." />

Response times depend on the plan. Check what you are entitled to before you need it — a production incident is a poor moment to discover the organisation has no plan.

## Raising a ticket

With a plan in place, select **New ticket** in the **Support** section.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/create-support-ticket.png" alt="The Support section of an organisation in SurrealDB Studio with the New ticket action available." />

Fill in a title and a description, and attach files if they help — logs, a query that reproduces the problem, a screenshot of an error.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/create-support-ticket-form.png" alt="The support ticket form in SurrealDB Studio, with fields for the ticket title and description and an attachment control." />

Include the [instance id](/docs/manage/instances/configure#the-instance-id) and the [organisation id](/docs/manage/organisations#organisation-settings) — both are copyable from Studio, and both remove a round trip. State what you expected, what happened, and when it started.

Submitting takes you to the ticket, where replies appear as the team works through it.

<img src="~/assets/img/image/cloud/create-support-ticket-page.png" alt="A submitted support ticket in SurrealDB Studio, showing its details and the thread of replies." />

**All tickets** in the top right of the Support section lists everything open and closed for the organisation.

## Account and billing questions

Billing and account queries go to [support@surrealdb.com](mailto:support@surrealdb.com), or through the Support section, and do not need a paid plan. Write from the address associated with your account. See [Billing](/docs/manage/organisations/billing).

## Reporting a security issue

Security reports go to [security@surrealdb.com](mailto:security@surrealdb.com), not to Discord or a ticket. For vulnerabilities in SurrealDB itself, use [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/surrealdb/surrealdb/security/advisories) rather than a public issue. The disclosure policy is in the [FAQs](/docs/manage/organisations/faqs#how-do-i-report-a-security-issue).
