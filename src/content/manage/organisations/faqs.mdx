# Frequently asked questions

## General

### What do I get with a managed instance?

A SurrealDB deployment that SurrealDB operates. Provisioning, high availability, patching, backups, and version upgrades are handled for you, so the infrastructure work is ours rather than yours. See [Instances](/docs/manage/instances) for what that covers, and [Self-hosted](/docs/manage/self-hosted) if you would rather run the server yourself.

### How do I get started?

[Create an account](/docs/manage/organisations/sign-in) at [app.surrealdb.com](https://app.surrealdb.com), [create an organisation](/docs/manage/organisations), and [deploy an instance](/docs/manage/instances/create). The free instance type needs no payment details.

### Which cloud platforms can I deploy to?

AWS, in the regions listed in [Create an instance](/docs/manage/instances/create). Support for further cloud providers is planned.

### Which programming languages are supported?

Any language with a SurrealDB SDK:

- [JavaScript and TypeScript](/docs/reference/javascript)
- [Python](/docs/reference/python)
- [Go](/docs/reference/golang)
- [Java](/docs/reference/java)
- [Rust](/docs/reference/rust)
- [PHP](/docs/reference/php)
- [.NET](/docs/reference/dotnet)

Anything else can use the [HTTP API](/docs/manage/instances/connect/via-http).

### Can I move an existing SurrealDB application onto a managed instance?

Yes. Export from your current deployment and import into the instance — see [Import and export](/docs/manage/instances/import-and-export), and [Migrating to SurrealDB](/docs/build/migrating) for moving from another database.

A dedicated migration tool for existing self-hosted deployments is in development. Until then, `surreal export` and `surreal import` are the route.

### Why am I getting an authentication error when connecting through an SDK?

The instance most likely has no user or access method defined yet. Studio authenticates with your own session, so it works before any credentials exist; an SDK does not. See [Connect via SDK](/docs/manage/instances/connect/via-sdk#create-credentials).

### Are my instances backed up?

Yes. Automated snapshots run on a schedule, with tiered daily, weekly, and monthly retention. How often snapshots are taken and how long each tier is kept depends on the instance type — configurable frequency is available on Scale instances. See [Backups and recovery](/docs/manage/instances/backups).

### How often can I increase the disk size of an instance?

Once every six hours. Storage can be increased but never decreased — see [Configure an instance](/docs/manage/instances/configure#compute-and-storage).

### Can I transfer an instance to another organisation?

No. Deploy an instance in the target organisation and move the data with [`surreal export` and `surreal import`](/docs/manage/instances/import-and-export).

### Can I delete an organisation?

Not currently. Delete the instances inside it and it stops accruing charges. See [Organisations](/docs/manage/organisations).

### What are the current limits?

- **Server flags and environment variables** cannot be set directly. Runtime behaviour is controlled through [instance capabilities](/docs/manage/instances/configure#capabilities) instead, in Studio or with `surrealctl instance capabilities`.
- **[GraphQL](/docs/learn/querying/graphql/overview)** is not yet enabled on managed instances.
- **Free instances** that exceed the 1 GB storage allowance become read-only. Existing data stays available; new writes are refused until you move to a paid instance type.
- **Request sizes** are capped per endpoint — see [request size limits](/docs/manage/instances/connect/via-http#request-size-limits).

### Where are the documentation and tutorials?

- [SurrealDB documentation](/docs) — guides and reference, including [SurrealDB Studio](/docs/explore/studio).
- [SurrealDB University](/learn) — a self-paced course with hands-on labs.
- [SurrealDB Book](/learn/book) — an in-depth guide to the database.
- [YouTube channel](https://youtube.com/surrealdb) — release walkthroughs and tutorials.

## Security

### How secure is the platform?

Instances run with encryption at rest and in transit, network isolation, access controls, and monitoring, as set out in our [Security Addendum](/legal/security-addendum). Multi-factor authentication is available through whichever identity provider you sign in with.

We hold ISO 27001, SOC 2 (Type 2), and Cyber Essentials Plus certifications, with further industry-specific programmes such as HIPAA and PCI DSS planned. The [Trust Centre](https://trust.surrealdb.com/) has the current detail.

Beyond the platform, instances inherit SurrealDB's own [security features](/docs/learn/security/authentication/summary#product) and the [security practices](/docs/learn/security/authentication/summary#process) the database is developed under. If you are assessing the service for a workload with specific requirements, [contact us](/contact).

### How do I report a security issue?

Email [security@surrealdb.com](mailto:security@surrealdb.com). For vulnerabilities in SurrealDB itself, submit a report through [GitHub Security Advisories](https://github.com/surrealdb/surrealdb/security/advisories) rather than opening a public issue.

We ask that you:

- Disclose the details privately to SurrealDB first.
- Include enough information to reproduce the issue.
- Do not disclose details publicly or to third parties before we have addressed them.
- Do not exploit the vulnerability beyond what is needed to verify it exists.
- Do not run automated security tools against our infrastructure without permission.

We commit to:

- Acknowledging your report within three business days.
- Verifying the issue and keeping you informed of progress.
- Treating your report and any data you share as confidential.
- Taking no legal action against you for a report made under this policy.
- Crediting you in any resulting advisory, unless you prefer otherwise.

### How do I configure user authentication?

Connect to the instance, then [define users](/docs/reference/query-language/statements/define/user) or [access methods](/docs/reference/query-language/statements/define/access) at root, namespace, or database level. Those credentials work across every interface: the [HTTP API](/docs/reference/rest-api), the [CLI](/docs/reference/cli/surrealdb-cli/overview), the SDKs, and [SurrealDB Studio](https://app.surrealdb.com). See [Connect via SDK](/docs/manage/instances/connect/via-sdk#create-credentials).

### Can I use my own encryption keys?

Not currently. Keys used to encrypt data at rest are managed for you. The architecture supports customer-managed encryption keys, so we may offer them in future — [contact us](/contact) if you need them.

### Can I control who reaches my instances over the network?

Instances are reachable over the public internet by default, protected by the authentication and authorisation defined inside them.

Two additional controls exist:

- **[AWS PrivateLink](/docs/manage/instances/private-connectivity)** gives an instance a private network path from your AWS VPC. In `private` mode no public hostname is assigned at all. It is available in a subset of regions and requires onboarding by the SurrealDB team.
- **[Instance capabilities](/docs/manage/instances/configure#capabilities)** restrict what the engine will do — which RPC methods and HTTP endpoints are served, whether guest access is permitted, and which [outbound destinations](/docs/manage/instances/network-access) a query may reach.

## Pricing

### How much does it cost?

You pay for what you use — compute consumed and storage provisioned. Current rates are on the [pricing page](/pricing), and [Usage](/docs/manage/organisations/billing#usage-and-spend) shows what an organisation has accrued so far this month.

### Is there a free tier?

Yes. The free instance type carries no charge and provides limited resources for development and evaluation. It has a 1 GB storage allowance, cannot expand its storage, and is paused automatically after seven days without use.

### I am on the free tier. Can I add another instance?

You will be prompted to add [billing information](/docs/manage/organisations/billing) to the organisation first.

### How am I billed?

Monthly, for the previous month's usage. The cycle runs from the first day of the month to the last, and the invoice arrives by email. See [Billing](/docs/manage/organisations/billing).

### Are invoices itemised, and is tax identified?

Yes. Enter your VAT or tax identification number in the organisation's billing details and it appears on every invoice.

### Are there additional fees?

No. All costs are on the [pricing page](/pricing). Prices quoted on the website exclude VAT and sales tax; applicable taxes are shown on the invoice.

### Who do I contact about billing?

Email [support@surrealdb.com](mailto:support@surrealdb.com) from the address associated with your account, describing the query.

## Legal

### What terms govern my use of the service?

Use of SurrealDB Cloud is governed by our Master Services Agreement, available on the [legal page](/legal) along with our compliance and privacy documentation.

### Do you serve US-embargoed countries or Russia?

SurrealDB Inc. complies with U.S. regulations on embargoed countries and regions, and currently prohibits use of its products and services in Cuba, Iran, North Korea, Syria, and the Russian-controlled regions of Crimea, the Luhansk People's Republic (LNR), and the Donetsk People's Republic (DNR). We use geoblocking to prevent logins from restricted regions; a regional block may restrict an entire country, which is **beyond our direct control**.

SurrealDB Ltd is also subject to Article 5n(2b) of Council Regulation 833/2014, which prohibits supplying software for the management of enterprises, or for design and manufacture, to the Government of Russia or to entities established in Russia.

These obligations are reviewed and updated as regulations change.

## Troubleshooting

### I cannot connect to my instance

Work through these in order:

1. Confirm the instance is **running** and not [paused](/docs/manage/instances/configure#pause-an-instance) — a paused instance is unreachable, and free instances pause themselves after seven days.
2. Check the **endpoint, namespace, and database** against the instance's Connect menu in Studio.
3. Check your **credentials** and that the user or access method has permission on that namespace and database.
4. If you are on [PrivateLink](/docs/manage/instances/private-connectivity), confirm you are connecting from inside the VPC — private hostnames do not resolve outside it.
5. Check whether the failing operation depends on a [capability](/docs/manage/instances/configure#capabilities) that is denied, such as an outbound HTTP call.
6. Check [metrics and logs](/docs/manage/instances/monitoring) for connection saturation or errors around the time it started failing.

If it still fails, raise a [support ticket](/docs/manage/organisations/support) with the instance id.

### How do I report a bug or request a feature?

Raise a [support ticket](/docs/manage/organisations/support), or ask in the [Discord](https://discord.gg/surrealdb) community. Include steps to reproduce, the instance id, what you expected, and what happened instead.
