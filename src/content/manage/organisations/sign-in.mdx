# Accounts and sign-in

One account gets you into [SurrealDB Studio](https://app.surrealdb.com), where you manage [organisations](/docs/manage/organisations) and [instances](/docs/manage/instances). Creating it takes a minute and does not require payment details — the free instance type needs none.

Open [app.surrealdb.com](https://app.surrealdb.com) and continue to the authentication page to create an account or sign in.

## Choosing a sign-in method

Four methods are available. Use the same one every time: signing up with Google and later trying to sign in with the same address by email creates a second, separate account rather than recovering the first.

| Method | What happens | Suited to |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Google** | You authorise SurrealDB through Google's own sign-in page | Teams already on Google Workspace |
| **GitHub** | You authorise SurrealDB through GitHub's own sign-in page | Developers signed in to GitHub already |
| **Email and password** | You set a password and confirm the address by clicking a verification link | Anyone who would rather not link a third-party account |
| **Passkey** | Your device authenticates you with biometrics or a device PIN, with no password to store | The strongest option where your device supports it |

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Google and GitHub sign-in uses OAuth2. You are redirected to the provider's own page, so your password is never sent to us. We request only your email address and name. Once you authorise the connection, the provider returns a token that confirms your identity.

Any two-factor authentication configured with Google or GitHub applies here too, which is the simplest way to get a second factor on your SurrealDB account.

## Creating an account

**With Google or GitHub:** select **Sign up with Google** or **Sign up with GitHub**, choose the account when the provider's window appears, and allow the requested permissions. You are signed in immediately afterwards.

**With email:** select **Sign up with Email**, enter your address, then set a password or register a passkey. Confirm the address by clicking the verification link sent to your inbox.

## Signing in

Return to [app.surrealdb.com](https://app.surrealdb.com) and choose the method you registered with — **Continue with Google**, **Continue with GitHub**, **Sign in with Email**, or **Continue with Passkey**. Complete any two-factor step the provider asks for.

If you have forgotten an email password, use **Forgot password?** on the authentication page and follow the instructions sent to your address. Accounts created through Google or GitHub have no SurrealDB password to reset; recover the provider account instead.

## Accounts for a team

Each person needs their own account. Invite colleagues to the organisation rather than sharing credentials — that way permissions can differ, and removing someone who leaves does not require changing a password everyone else uses. See [Members and roles](/docs/manage/organisations/members-and-roles).

For automation, use a token from [`surrealctl`](/docs/manage/surrealctl/authentication) rather than an interactive account.
