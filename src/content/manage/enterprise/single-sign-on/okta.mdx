# Okta

Connect Okta to SurrealDB to allow members of your organisation to sign in with their Okta credentials. You build an app integration with the Okta Integration Wizard, then enter its details in a new connection in SurrealDB Studio.

When you finish, people can sign in to SurrealDB through Okta and open it from a tile on their Okta dashboard. You can also let Okta create and deactivate their accounts with [SCIM provisioning](/docs/manage/enterprise/single-sign-on/scim-provisioning).

Before you start, [verify the email domain](/docs/manage/enterprise/single-sign-on#step-1-verify-an-email-domain) your company uses. You also need an Okta account that can create app integrations.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> Use the **Okta Integration Wizard**, not the classic **OIDC - OpenID Connect** app type. Okta only lets you add SCIM provisioning to an OpenID Connect integration that you build with the wizard.

## Step 1: Build the app integration

1. In the Okta Admin Console, open **Applications and Resources**, then **Applications**.
2. Select **Create App Integration**.

![The Applications page in the Okta Admin Console, with the Create App Integration button highlighted.](~/assets/img/surrealdb/manage/enterprise-sso-okta-1.webp)

3. Select **Okta Integration Wizard**.
4. Enter a name for the integration, for example `SurrealDB`.
5. Add the **SSO** capability, and choose **OIDC** as its protocol.
6. Under the SSO settings, add `https://auth.surrealdb.com/login/callback` to **Redirect URIs**.
7. Deploy the integration.

The wizard makes a private integration in your org. Leave the **Initiate login URI** empty for now. SurrealDB gives you its value in step 4.

## Step 2: Add the app and copy its credentials

A deployed integration is a template. To use it, add an app from it.

1. Open the app catalog, then **Private Apps**.
2. Add the `SurrealDB` integration.
3. On the **Assignments** tab, assign the people or groups who can sign in.
4. On the **General** tab, under **Client Credentials**, copy the **Client ID**.
5. Under **Client secrets**, copy the secret. Select the eye icon to show it, or the copy icon beside it.

![The General tab of the SurrealDB app integration in Okta, with the client ID and the client secret highlighted.](~/assets/img/surrealdb/manage/enterprise-sso-okta-4.webp)

Only the people and groups assigned to the app can sign in to SurrealDB through it.

You also need your Okta domain, for example `example.okta.com`. It is the domain of your Okta sign-in page, without `-admin`.

## Step 3: Add the connection in SurrealDB

1. Open **Single sign-on** in your enterprise and add a new connection.
2. Select **Okta**.
3. Under **Name the connection**, enter a **Display name**. Members see this name on the sign-in screen.
4. Under **Enter the Okta details**, enter:
    - **Okta domain**: your Okta domain, for example `example.okta.com`.
    - **Client ID**: the client ID from step 2.
    - **Scopes**: optional. Leave it empty to request `openid profile email`.
    - **Client secret**: the client secret from step 2.
5. Under **Route sign-in domains**, select the verified domains to send to this connection. This is optional, and you can change it later.
6. Select **Create connection**.

![The New Okta connection form in SurrealDB Studio, with the display name, Okta domain, client ID, client secret and a routed domain filled in.](~/assets/img/surrealdb/manage/enterprise-sso-okta-5.webp)

SurrealDB stores the client secret securely and does not show it again.

## Step 4: Show SurrealDB on the Okta dashboard

The Okta dashboard shows a tile for each app a person is assigned. For the tile to open SurrealDB, Okta needs the address that starts a sign-in.

1. In Studio, open the connection, then the **Sign-on** tab.
2. Under **Set up Okta**, copy the **Initiate login URI**.
3. In Okta, edit the `SurrealDB` integration.
4. Under the SSO settings, paste the value into **Initiate login URI**.
5. Deploy the integration again.

When a person selects the tile, Okta sends them to SurrealDB, and SurrealDB signs them in through this connection. They do not have to enter their email address.
