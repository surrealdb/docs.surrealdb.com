# Enterprise teams & permissions

A team is a group of [members](/docs/manage/enterprise/members). Use teams to give many people the same enterprise permissions or the same access to the enterprise's organisations.

A team does not need permissions. A team with no permissions is a group of people that you can give access to organisations.

## Create a team

1. Open the **Teams** tab in your enterprise.
2. Select **Create team**.
3. Enter a **Name**.
4. Optionally, enter a **Description** that tells people what the team is for.
5. Select **Create team**.

Two teams can have the same name. A team page has these tabs:

| Tab | What you can do there |
| --- | --- |
| **Members** | See who is on the team, add and remove members, and choose the maintainers. |
| **Organisations** | Give the team access to the enterprise's organisations. |
| **Permissions** | Choose the enterprise permissions the team holds. |
| **Settings** | Change the name and description, copy the team ID, and delete the team. |

## Team members

A person is on a team in one or both of these ways:

- **Direct:** someone added them to the team on the **Members** tab.
- **Through a linked group:** they are in a SCIM group from your identity provider that is linked to the team. The **Members** tab shows the group beside their name.

Link groups to a team on the **Provisioning** tab of a single sign-on connection. See [SCIM provisioning](/docs/manage/enterprise/scim-provisioning#link-scim-groups-to-teams). A team can have more than one linked group.

**Remove** removes only a direct membership. If the person is also in a linked group, they stay on the team. A person who is on the team only through a group leaves when your identity provider removes them from the group, or when you unlink the group.

To add a person to a team, they must already be a member of the enterprise. To bring a new person into the enterprise, [invite them](/docs/manage/enterprise/members#invite-one-member).

## Maintainers

Any team member can be a maintainer. A maintainer does not need an enterprise permission to:

- add enterprise members to the team, and remove direct members;
- make other members maintainers, or stop them being maintainers;
- change the team's name and description.

A maintainer cannot change the team's permissions, organisation access or linked groups, and cannot delete the team.

> [!IMPORTANT]
> A person a maintainer adds gets everything the team holds, including its permissions and its access to organisations. Choose maintainers for teams with permissions or organisation access with care.

## Access to organisations

Membership of the enterprise alone gives no access to the enterprise's organisations. Each person or team gets access to an organisation explicitly, with the **admin** or **member** [organisation role](/docs/manage/organisations/members-and-roles).

On the team's **Organisations** tab, add an organisation and choose the role. Everyone on the team then gets that role in the organisation. When a person gets more than one role in the same organisation, they hold the strongest one.

A person who is already a member of the organisation in the ordinary way keeps that membership. Access from the enterprise does not change it.

## Permissions

The **Permissions** tab lists the enterprise permissions a team can hold, grouped by area. Everyone on the team holds the permissions you select.

| Area | Permissions | What they cover |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Members** | Read, add, update, remove | Seeing the member list, inviting people, changing who is on any team, and ending a membership. |
| **Teams** | Read, create, update, delete | Seeing teams, creating them, changing the permissions, linked groups and settings of any team, and deleting them. |
| **Domains** | Read, create, verify, delete | Seeing domains, adding one, verifying ownership, and removing one. |
| **Single sign-on** | Read, create, update, delete, route domains, set applications | Seeing connections, adding or removing one, choosing which domains route to it, and choosing which applications it may be used with. |
| **Organisations** | Read, create, manage access | Seeing the enterprise's organisations, creating one, and giving people and teams access to them. |
| **Settings** | Update | Changing the display name and the branding. |

Permission to read an area and permission to change it are granted separately. A team can therefore let someone audit single sign-on without letting them alter a connection.

> [!WARNING]
> Permission to update teams, update members or manage organisation access lets a person give themselves admin access to the enterprise's organisations. Grant these permissions only to people you trust with that access.

## The Admins team and the owner

Every enterprise has an **Admins** team that holds every permission. You cannot delete it.

Every enterprise has one owner, who holds every permission. The owner is always on the Admins team. To transfer ownership to a different member, use the dots menu in the member list.
